Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Kitchen & Baking Converter is designed to be private by default. This policy explains — in plain language — what data the app handles and what it never does.

1. Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. There is no server-side database storing user information.

2. Data stored on your device (local only)

To make the app useful, a small amount of information is saved only in your browser's local storage on your own device. It never leaves your device:

  • Your favorite conversions
  • Your saved recipe lines and batch multiplier
  • Your interface language (English / French / Spanish) and theme preference

Clearing this data: clearing your browser / app storage (or uninstalling the app) permanently removes everything above. No copy exists anywhere else.

3. Permissions

The app requests no sensitive Android permissions (no location, no camera, no microphone, no contacts, no storage).

4. Children

The app contains no age-restricted content and is suitable for general audiences. Since no data is collected, no data from children is processed.

5. Third-party services

None. The app does not embed third-party analytics, ads, or trackers. Web fonts and the app itself are served directly by the app's own host.

6. Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version adds optional cloud sync), the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact the developer through the email listed on the Google Play Store listing for this app.
